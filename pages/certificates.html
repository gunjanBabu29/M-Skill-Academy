Certificates That Can Be Verified

Students who successfully complete a course's assessment requirements receive an institute-issued Certificate of Completion.

What the Certificate Represents

Our certificate confirms that a student has completed the coursework, projects and assessments required by a specific program. It is an institute-issued Certificate of Completion — not a government or regulatory accreditation.

We do not claim Government Certification, UGC Approval, AICTE Approval, NCVET Certification or NSQF Certification unless such recognition genuinely applies. If we obtain any such recognition in future, this page will be updated to reflect it clearly.
