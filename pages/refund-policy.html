1. General Policy

Course fees and refund conditions may differ depending on the course, duration, batch, mode of training, promotional offer, admission category, and payment method.

The applicable refund terms communicated at the time of admission will apply, subject to applicable law.

2. Before Course Commencement

A student may request cancellation before the scheduled commencement of the course. Where eligible, the institute may provide a refund after deducting any applicable:

  • Non-refundable registration/admission charges
  • Administrative charges
  • Third-party payment processing charges actually incurred

The applicable refund amount will depend on the specific course and fee structure communicated at admission.

3. After Course Commencement

Once classes have commenced, refunds may be restricted because the institute may already have incurred costs relating to trainer allocation, classroom capacity, learning materials, administrative processing, batch reservation, and course resources.

Refund requests after course commencement will therefore be evaluated according to the applicable course-specific refund terms.

4. Registration/Admission Fee

If a separate registration or admission fee is clearly identified as non-refundable before payment, that fee will generally not be refundable except where required by applicable law or where the institute expressly agrees otherwise.

5. Institute-Cancelled Course or Batch

If the institute cancels a course or batch before it begins and does not provide a reasonable alternative batch or equivalent solution, the student may be eligible for a refund of the amount paid for the cancelled course, subject to applicable terms and law.

The institute may alternatively offer transfer to another batch, transfer to another equivalent program, or rescheduling, with the student's agreement.

6. Batch Transfer

Where operationally feasible, a student may request transfer to another batch. Batch transfers may be subject to availability, course compatibility, administrative conditions, and difference in course fees, where applicable.

A batch transfer does not automatically create a right to a cash refund.

7. Student Absence

Failure to attend classes because of personal reasons, travel, employment, examinations, illness, or other circumstances does not automatically entitle a student to a refund.

Where appropriate, the institute may offer makeup classes, recorded material, batch transfer, or academic support depending on course structure and availability.

8. Medical or Exceptional Circumstances

The institute may consider refund, deferral, or batch-transfer requests arising from exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis. Supporting documentation may be requested where reasonably necessary. Approval is not automatic.

9. Duplicate or Incorrect Payment

If a student accidentally makes a duplicate payment for the same course, the student should contact the institute as soon as possible. After verification, the duplicate amount may be refunded or adjusted against applicable fees.

10. Failed or Reversed Transactions

If an amount is debited from a student's account but the institute does not receive the payment successfully, the student should provide the relevant transaction reference. The institute may verify the transaction with the payment provider or bank. Refunds or reversals may be subject to the timelines of the relevant banking/payment system.

11. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the institute will generally process it to the original payment method where reasonably possible. The refund may take additional time to appear in the student's bank/card account because of payment gateway or banking processing timelines.

The institute is not responsible for delays caused solely by banks or third-party payment providers.

12. Promotional and Discounted Fees

Special promotional offers, scholarships, early-bird discounts, referral discounts, corporate discounts, or other discounted fees may have specific refund conditions. Such conditions will be communicated before payment where applicable.

13. No Refund for Completed Services

Where a student has completed a substantial portion of a course, accessed substantial course resources, completed assessments, or received the principal benefit of the service, refund eligibility may be limited subject to the applicable course-specific terms and law.

14. Certificate Fees

Where an external examination, certification, assessment, re-examination, examination registration, or third-party certificate involves a separate fee, that fee may be subject to the external provider's refund policy.

15. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us using the details in Section 18 below. Please include:

  • Student name
  • Registered mobile number
  • Registered email address
  • Course/program name
  • Admission/registration ID
  • Payment date
  • Payment reference/transaction ID
  • Reason for refund request
Do not send passwords, UPI PINs, card PINs, CVV numbers, or other payment credentials.

16. Refund Review

Refund requests will be reviewed by the institute based on applicable course policy, date of request, course commencement status, services already provided, payment records, relevant circumstances, and applicable law.

The institute may request additional information reasonably necessary to process the request.

17. Changes to Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with the revised "Last Updated" date.

The policy applicable to an existing enrolment will generally be determined according to the terms communicated to the student at the time of enrolment, subject to applicable law.
