At Nexora Skills & Career Academy ("NEXORA", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information provided by students, prospective students, parents/guardians, website visitors, trainers, and other individuals who interact with our institute.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us, enquire about our courses, register for a program, or otherwise interact with us.

By using our website or providing your information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Date of birth, where required
  • Gender, where voluntarily provided
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Residential/correspondence address
  • City and state
  • Emergency contact information, where applicable

1.2 Educational and Career Information

For course admission, counselling, placement assistance, or career-readiness activities, we may collect:

  • Educational qualifications
  • College/institution details
  • Graduation year
  • Current employment status
  • Work experience
  • Technical and professional skills
  • Career interests
  • Target job role
  • Resume/CV, where voluntarily submitted

1.3 Course and Admission Information

We may collect:

  • Course/program selected
  • Batch details
  • Mode of training
  • Admission/registration information
  • Attendance information
  • Assessment results
  • Assignment/project information
  • Certificate information

1.4 Payment Information

Where applicable, we may receive information relating to course payments, such as:

  • Transaction reference number
  • Payment amount
  • Payment date
  • Payment status
  • Payment method

We generally do not need to store complete debit-card, credit-card, banking passwords, UPI PINs, or similar sensitive payment credentials on our own systems. Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers, whose own terms and privacy policies may apply.

1.5 Website and Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referral/source information
  • Website interaction information

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

  • Submit an enquiry form
  • Request a counselling session
  • Contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or other communication channels
  • Register for a course
  • Complete an admission form
  • Make a payment
  • Submit a resume/CV
  • Participate in assessments
  • Request a certificate
  • Request certificate verification
  • Subscribe to communications
  • Visit or interact with our website

We may also receive information from a parent/guardian or authorized representative where appropriate.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for legitimate purposes connected with our educational and training activities, including:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Providing course information
  • Scheduling counselling sessions
  • Processing admissions
  • Managing student registrations
  • Creating student records
  • Managing batches and attendance
  • Delivering training
  • Conducting assessments
  • Managing projects and assignments
  • Issuing certificates
  • Verifying certificates
  • Providing career guidance
  • Providing career assistance and placement-support activities
  • Communicating course-related information
  • Processing payments and refunds
  • Sending important administrative notices
  • Improving our courses and services
  • Maintaining website security
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We will not use your personal information for purposes materially different from those communicated to you without an appropriate legal basis or notice where required.

4. Marketing Communications

Where permitted and appropriate, we may contact you regarding: new courses, new batches, workshops, seminars, career events, training offers, and institute announcements.

You may request that we stop sending promotional communications. Course-related or legally necessary communications may still be sent even if you opt out of promotional communications.

5. WhatsApp and Other Communication Services

If you contact us through WhatsApp or another third-party communication platform, information exchanged through that platform may also be subject to the platform's own terms and privacy policy.

We recommend that you do not send unnecessary sensitive information through WhatsApp, email, or public website forms.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the website functioning properly, remember certain preferences, understand website usage, improve website performance, analyze traffic, and improve user experience.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website or institute may use third-party services such as website hosting providers, domain providers, email service providers, payment gateways, analytics services, communication platforms, cloud storage providers, CAPTCHA/security services, website forms, and social media platforms.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual/legal requirements. We do not sell personal information as a commercial product.

8. Data Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our institute and provide our services. Examples may include:

  • Payment processors
  • Website/hosting providers
  • Email providers
  • IT/service providers
  • Authorized trainers
  • Professional advisers
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required
  • Emergency services where reasonably necessary

We will seek to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Student Photographs, Videos and Testimonials

We may request permission to use photographs, videos, testimonials, project showcases, or student achievements for legitimate institute activities and promotional purposes. We will seek appropriate consent where required.

You may contact us to ask about withdrawal of consent for future promotional use. Withdrawal will not necessarily affect materials already lawfully published before the withdrawal request.

10. Children's and Minor Students' Information

Our institute primarily provides programs for students and adults who are legally able to enrol. Where we knowingly process personal information relating to a child or another individual requiring consent/authorization under applicable law, we will follow applicable legal requirements and seek consent from the appropriate parent, guardian, or authorized person where required.

Parents/guardians may contact us regarding information relating to a minor enrolled with the institute.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, computer system, email transmission, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also take reasonable precautions when sharing information online.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including:

  • Course administration
  • Student records
  • Certificate verification
  • Accounting and taxation
  • Legal compliance
  • Dispute resolution
  • Security and fraud prevention

Certain records may need to be retained for longer periods because of legal, accounting, regulatory, or legitimate institutional requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request information about processing of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion/erasure where legally applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise a complaint or grievance
  • Request information regarding the handling of your personal data

Requests can be made using the contact details in Section 17 below. Applicable rights and the manner in which they may be exercised can depend on the nature of the information and the applicable law.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework provides for rights and obligations concerning digital personal data, with the 2025 Rules establishing the implementation framework and phased commencement.

14. Certificate Verification

Our website may provide a certificate verification facility. Information displayed through certificate verification may include limited information such as: Certificate ID, student name, course/program, duration, completion status, and issue date.

We will seek to avoid displaying unnecessary personal information through a publicly accessible verification page.

15. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, applicable law, or our data practices. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
